# ADR 0363: The explainer is "pick your lens", and its game lens is the user's judgment, not the crew

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-28

Chart mc-tmuh9 (public mugicrew explainer site with three framings). Settles
prototype mc-tmuh9.4, whose throwaway outline (side ref
`refs/mugicrew/prototypes/mc-tmuh9`) the human tried in three structures.

## Decision

1. **Structure: pick your lens.** The explainer opens with a hero, then offers
   the three lenses as three doors into one explanation, in this order:
   problem-solving workspace, technical framework, creative strategy game (the
   human's call after mc-tmuh9.7, so the doors, nav and cross-links lead with
   the problem a stranger brings). Each lens is a full path on its own and ends
   at the same Try it; each links across to the other two.
2. **The game lens describes the user's experience, not the crew.** Building
   with mugicrew is like playing a strategy game in which you manifest your
   requirements through your judgment: the bead graph is the map, open
   questions are fog your answers clear, your moves are calls (which Signals to
   chase, grilling answers, prototype picks, approve or send back, withdraw or
   roll back), and winning is your requirements landing as commits. The game
   lens carries no crewmates and no crew role-to-step mapping along the spine.
3. **Outline content is otherwise accepted as prototyped**: the hero, the other
   two lenses' sections, Try it, and the flagged diagram and Charm VHS spots.

## Considered Options

- **Three acts in a row** (variant A: hero, each lens in turn, Try it) and
  **one spine, three lenses per step** (variant B: each spine step once, the
  three lenses side by side). Rejected after the try: C read best.
- **Crew role-to-step mapping in the game lens**, with the human as captain.
  Rejected: it explained the crew rather than the experience, and it raised a
  needless tension between the human as captain and Luffy as the crew's final
  adjudicator.

## Consequences

The Chart's earlier note that the One Piece crew metaphor carries the game
lens, and that the Spec carries a crew role-to-step mapping, no longer hold.
Crewmate names may still appear where the site explains mechanics, never as
artwork.
