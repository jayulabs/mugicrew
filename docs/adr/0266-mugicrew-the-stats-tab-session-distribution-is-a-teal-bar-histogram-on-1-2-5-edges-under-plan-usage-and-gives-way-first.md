# The Stats tab session distribution is a teal bar histogram on 1-2-5 edges under Plan usage, and gives way first

Placement and short-height rules are amended by ADR 0297 (mugicrew-the-stats-tab-flows-usage-sessions-distribution…) and ADR 0298 (mugicrew-the-stats-flow-sizes-sessions-to-its-natural-width…).

This extends ADR 0259 (mugicrew-the-stats-tab-burn-chart-is-a-block-area-chart…). The Stats tab shows one session frequency histogram at a time, either Agent time or Session cost. It was settled by prototype (bead "Distribution rendering, bucket edges and placement in the Stats tab", throwaway on `refs/mugicrew/prototypes/mc-85c0.9`, variant A with C's colour), against the real event log.

- **Edges: a fixed 1-2-5 log ladder.** Agent time: `<1m 1–2m 2–5m 5–10m 10–30m 30m–1h 1h+`. Session cost: `$0 <0.25 0.25–0.50 0.50–1 1–2.50 $2.50+`. A bucket is [lower, upper). `$0` holds only known zero-cost sessions, and a session with no `dur_ms` falls in `<1m`. On the log from 2026-09-29 to 10-17, the largest bucket holds about 40% of sessions, the smallest about 1%, and no bucket is empty, over 7d or over all time. The starting edges (`1–5m`, `<$0.10` … `$5+`) were rejected: `1–5m` held 54%, and every session in `<$0.10` cost exactly $0, so the label was misleading.
- **Block.** A bold accent **Distribution** header, then a dim head line naming the shown histogram, its session count and the toggle: `agent time · 5243 sessions · d session cost`. Then one row per bucket: the label padded to the longest label, an eighth-block bar (`▏▎▍▌▋▊▉█`), and the count right-aligned. A non-empty bucket always shows at least a `▏` sliver. A zero count is dim.
- **Scaling and colour.** The largest bucket fills the bar width, which is whatever the column leaves after the label and the count. Bars are teal (256-colour 109), so they do not read as the burn chart's orange 180 or its warning colours. Share-of-all scaling with a `%` column, and %-only rows, were rejected.
- **Cost footer.** Under the cost histogram goes a dim `+N sessions without a cost`, counting that histogram's own cost-unknown sessions. Those sessions are absent from the cost bars but still counted under Agent time.
- **Placement.** _Superseded by ADR 0297 and ADR 0298: the Distribution is the third block of a flow, 30 to 46 columns wide, and wrapped alone it stays left-aligned at most 46 wide. It no longer sits in a right column below the burn chart or under the table at 72 columns._
- **Short heights.** The Distribution gives way first, then the burn chart, then Sessions rows (ADR 0259). The histogram is all or nothing: when it does not fit whole, it is replaced by a single dim `(distribution hidden: too short)`, because a histogram with buckets cut off misreads. The burn chart then shrinks by ADR 0259's rules.
